# UGC Ad Scripts - The Voyage

## Script 1: Problem → Solution → CTA

**Duration:** ~30 seconds

### Spoken Dialogue

**Hook:**
"Can we talk about how difficult it can be to choose a cafe for your next hangout in KP?"

**Problem:**
"You want somewhere to catch up with friends, grab a coffee, and actually enjoy your time, but choosing the right place can be a struggle."

**Solution:**
"That's where The Voyage in Koregaon Park comes into the picture. If you're looking for a cafe to add to your Pune list, this is one place you can check out."

**CTA:**
"Save this reel for your next cafe plan in KP and send it to your hangout buddy."

### On-Screen Text

* Planning a hangout in KP? ☕
* Looking for a cafe to try?
* The Voyage, Koregaon Park 📍
* Save this for your next plan!

### Visuals

* Creator checking their phone while deciding where to go
* Exterior/entrance of The Voyage
* Cafe interior
* Food or beverage close-ups
* Friends sitting and talking
* Final location shot
