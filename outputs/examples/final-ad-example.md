# Final UGC Ad Example - The Voyage

## Platform

Instagram Reels

## Duration

Approximately 30 seconds

## Content Style

Natural UGC-style cafe advertisement

## Target Audience

College students, young professionals, friends and couples in Pune.

---

## Hook

**"Looking for a cafe to add to your Pune list? Here's one you can check out."**

---

## Spoken Script

**Hook:**
Looking for a cafe to add to your Pune list? Here's one you can check out.

**Problem:**
Finding a cafe for your next coffee plan or hangout in Koregaon Park can sometimes take a lot of scrolling.

**Solution:**
The Voyage is located in Koregaon Park, Pune, and can be another option to consider when you're planning a coffee, catch-up or casual outing.

**CTA:**
Save this Reel for your next cafe plan and share it with someone you'd like to visit with.

---

## Visual Direction

### Scene 1 - Hook

Creator speaks directly to the camera while introducing the cafe.

### Scene 2 - Problem

Show the creator browsing cafe options on a phone.

### Scene 3 - Cafe Introduction

Show the exterior or entrance of The Voyage.

### Scene 4 - Cafe Content

Show available interior, seating, food or beverage visuals.

### Scene 5 - CTA

End with the creator or a location shot while displaying the CTA on screen.

**"Save this Reel for your next cafe plan and share it with someone you'd like to visit with."**

## Content Notes

* UGC-style conversational language
* Short-form video friendly
* Problem → Solution → CTA structure
* No fabricated personal experience
* No unsupported prices, discounts or offers
* No unsupported claims about facilities or food quality
