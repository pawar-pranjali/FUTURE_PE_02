# AI Content Marketing using UGC Ads

## Future Interns - Prompt Engineering Task 2 (2026)

### Project Overview

This project demonstrates the use of AI-powered prompt engineering to create UGC-style advertising content for a real local business.

The selected business is **The Voyage**, a cafe located in Koregaon Park, Pune.

The content is designed primarily for **Instagram Reels** and focuses on creating natural, conversational and engaging UGC-style advertisements.

## Problem Being Addressed

People exploring cafes in Koregaon Park may have difficulty deciding where to go for:

* Coffee plans
* Casual catch-ups
* Meeting friends
* Casual dates
* Exploring new cafes in Pune

The content uses short-form UGC advertising to introduce The Voyage as another cafe option for people exploring the area.

---

## AI Prompt Strategy

The project uses structured prompts to generate different parts of the UGC advertising content.

The prompts focus on:

1. Creating attention-grabbing hooks
2. Building short UGC ad scripts
3. Using the Problem → Solution → CTA framework
4. Creating natural calls-to-action
5. Writing Instagram-ready captions
6. Providing visual directions for short-form videos

The prompts are designed to avoid exaggerated claims, fake personal experiences and unsupported offers or facilities.

---

## Content Framework

### Hook

The first few seconds are designed to capture attention using:

* Discovery
* Relatable problems
* Curiosity
* Recommendations
* Lifestyle situations

### Problem

The content presents a relatable situation such as finding a cafe for a coffee plan or catch-up.

### Solution

The Voyage is introduced as a cafe that people can consider adding to their Pune cafe list.

### CTA

The audience is encouraged to:

* Save the Reel
* Share it
* Tag friends
* Comment
* Consider visiting the cafe

## UGC Ad Formats Created

### 1. Problem → Solution → CTA

Introduces a common cafe-planning problem, presents The Voyage as an option and ends with an action-oriented CTA.

### 2. Discovery → Recommendation → CTA

Introduces the cafe and encourages viewers to consider adding it to their Pune cafe list.

### 3. Before → After → CTA

Shows the process of searching for a cafe and presents The Voyage as another option to consider.

## Content Guidelines

The generated content follows these guidelines:

* Keep the language natural and conversational
* Avoid overly promotional language
* Keep scripts suitable for short-form video
* Focus on audience problems and solutions
* Use clear calls-to-action
* Avoid fabricated reviews or personal experiences
* Avoid unsupported prices, discounts or offers
* Avoid unsupported claims about facilities or services
