# Hook Prompt - UGC Ad Hooks

## Prompt

Create five short and engaging UGC-style hooks for an Instagram Reel promoting **The Voyage**, a cafe in Koregaon Park, Pune.

### Business Details

* **Business:** The Voyage
* **Type:** Cafe
* **Location:** Koregaon Park, Pune
* **Target Audience:** College students, young professionals, friends and couples
* **Platform:** Instagram Reels
* **Style:** Natural UGC
* **Tone:** Friendly, casual and conversational

### Requirements

Generate **5 different hooks**.

Each hook should:

* Grab attention within the first few seconds
* Sound like something a real person would say
* Be short and easy to speak
* Be suitable for an Instagram Reel
* Use a different angle

Use these angles:

1. Discovery
2. Relatable problem
3. Curiosity
4. Recommendation
5. Lifestyle

### Content Rules

* Do not use exaggerated claims.
* Do not invent personal experiences.
* Do not create fake reviews.
* Do not mention prices, discounts or offers unless provided.
* Do not claim specific facilities or menu items unless provided.
* Keep the hooks natural rather than overly promotional.
