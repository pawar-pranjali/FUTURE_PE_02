# Script Prompt - UGC Ad Scripts

## Prompt

Act as an expert UGC ad copywriter and create three short-form UGC advertising scripts for **The Voyage**, a cafe in Koregaon Park, Pune.

### Business Details

* **Business:** The Voyage
* **Business Type:** Cafe
* **Location:** Koregaon Park, Pune
* **Target Audience:** College students, young professionals, friends and couples
* **Platform:** Instagram Reels
* **Style:** Natural UGC
* **Tone:** Friendly, casual and conversational
* **Approximate Duration:** 30 seconds per script

### Generate Three Scripts

#### Script 1: Problem → Solution → CTA

Create a relatable problem related to choosing a cafe for a coffee plan, catch-up or casual outing.

Introduce The Voyage as an option to consider.

End with a natural CTA.

#### Script 2: Discovery → Recommendation → CTA

Introduce The Voyage as a cafe in Koregaon Park.

Explain why someone exploring cafes in Pune might consider adding it to their list.

End with a natural CTA.

#### Script 3: Before → After → CTA

Show the situation before finding another cafe option.

Then introduce The Voyage as another option for a coffee, catch-up or casual outing.

End with a natural CTA.

### For Each Script Include

1. **Spoken Dialogue**
2. **On-Screen Text**
3. **Visual Directions**
4. **CTA**

### Content Guidelines

* Keep each script approximately 30 seconds.
* Make the dialogue sound natural when spoken.
* Keep the language simple and conversational.
* Make the scripts suitable for Instagram Reels.
* Avoid overly promotional language.
* Do not invent prices, discounts, offers or menu items.
* Do not invent facilities or business features.
* Do not create fake personal experiences.
* Do not create fake reviews.
* Do not make unsupported claims.
* Focus on the problem → solution → CTA structure.
