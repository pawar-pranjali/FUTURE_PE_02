# Master Prompt - UGC Ad Content

## Prompt

Act as an expert UGC ad copywriter and content marketing strategist.

Create a short-form UGC advertising content pack for the following business:

**Business:** The Voyage
**Business Type:** Cafe
**Location:** Koregaon Park, Pune
**Target Audience:** College students, young professionals, friends and couples
**Primary Platform:** Instagram Reels
**Style:** Authentic, natural UGC
**Tone:** Friendly, casual and conversational
**Goal:** Increase awareness, cafe visits and enquiries

### Generate:

1. **Five attention-grabbing hooks**

   * Short and natural
   * Suitable for Instagram Reels
   * Use different angles such as discovery, curiosity, relatable problems and lifestyle
   * Avoid exaggerated claims

2. **One approximately 30-second UGC ad script**

   * Follow the Problem → Solution → CTA structure
   * Use natural spoken dialogue
   * Include on-screen text suggestions
   * Include simple visual directions

3. **Five natural CTAs**

   * Encourage actions such as saving, sharing, commenting or considering a visit
   * Keep them suitable for Instagram Reels
   * Do not mention unsupported discounts, offers or booking options

4. **One Instagram caption**

   * Friendly and conversational
   * Mention The Voyage and Koregaon Park, Pune
   * Include a natural CTA
   * Include relevant hashtags

### Content Guidelines

* Make the content sound like a real person speaking naturally.
* Keep the language simple and conversational.
* Avoid overly promotional language.
* Do not invent prices, discounts, offers, facilities or menu items.
* Do not create fake personal experiences or fake reviews.
* Do not make unsupported claims about the business.
* Keep the content suitable for short-form social media advertising.
* Clearly separate hooks, script, CTAs and caption.
